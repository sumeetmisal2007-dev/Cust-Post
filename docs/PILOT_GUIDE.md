# Cust-Post Pilot Program Guide

Welcome to the **Cust-Post** student & urban resident pilot program!

Cust-Post is an evidence-first, space-aware composting assistant engineered to make household organic waste processing safe, odorless, and reliable in compact urban dwellings (balconies, utility corridors, terraces).

---

## 1. Core Principles & Guardrails

- **Zero Black-Box AI Verdicts:** Every recommendation and troubleshooting instruction is computed by deterministic rule modules citing published open agronomic and municipal literature (US EPA, MCGM, ADEQ, FAO).
- **Geometric Volume $\neq$ Biological Capacity:** Bin dimensions establish structural space clearance, not biological load. Never overload a container.
- **Physical Maturity Gate:** Compost cannot be marked ready by calendar date alone. All 4 observable maturity tests (color, odor, temperature, scrap breakdown) must pass before application.
- **20–30% Blending Rule:** Never plant seeds or seedlings in 100% pure compost. Always blend mature compost into 70–80% garden soil or coco peat.

---

## 2. Quick Setup Walkthrough

1. **Space Profile:** Measure available space in centimeters ($L \times W \times \text{Height}$).
2. **Container Screening:**
   - Measure internal dimensions.
   - Specify previous contents. *Caution:* Containers previously containing paint, motor oil, or industrial pesticides are strictly rejected.
   - Unknown provenance will prompt for inspection before clearing.
3. **Materials Inventory:** Select typical weekly kitchen greens (vegetable peels, tea leaves) and browns (dry leaves, shredded cardboard).
4. **Preferences:** Set your weekly turning capacity and smell sensitivity.
5. **Review & Recommendation:** Receive an explainable verdict (`suitable`, `suitable_with_changes`, `unsuitable`, or `more_info_needed`).
6. **Provenance Check:** Click *"Why this recommendation?"* to inspect the exact literature citations and reviewer notes.

---

## 3. Active Batch Tracking Routine

Once you click **Start Active Batch**:
- **Day 1:** Add brown bedding layer (dry leaves / shredded cardboard), add drained food scraps, cover with brown carbon blanket.
- **Every 3–4 Days:** Open bin and perform a 60-second observation:
  - Check moisture: Aim for a "wrung-out damp sponge" consistency.
  - Check odor: Should smell like fresh earth or damp forest.
  - Turn/fluff: Mix the top layers to introduce oxygen.
  - Log observation in Cust-Post: Takes 15 seconds on mobile.

---

## 4. Handling Common Problems (Troubleshooter)

If anything smells off or attracts gnats:
1. Open your batch dashboard in Cust-Post.
2. Tap **[Troubleshoot Issue]**.
3. Select your symptom:
   - **Sour / Ammonia smell:** Aerate immediately and blend in 2–3 handfuls of dry shredded cardboard.
   - **Fruit flies / Gnats:** Ensure zero food scraps are exposed; add a 2cm thick brown cover cap.
   - **Soggy sludge:** Check bottom drainage holes for blockage.
4. Tap **[Apply & Record in Batch Log]** to automatically record the corrective action in your timeline.

---

## 5. Harvesting Your Compost (Maturity Checklist)

When your batch approaches its readiness window:
1. Tap **[Maturity Checklist]** from the dashboard.
2. Verify the 4 mandatory observable criteria:
   - [ ] **Dark Crumbly Texture:** Deep brown/black spongy soil texture.
   - [ ] **Earthy Odor:** Zero rotting, vinegary, or ammonia smell.
   - [ ] **Ambient Temperature:** Pile core has cooled to ambient outdoor air for $\ge 7$ days.
   - [ ] **Breakdown:** Original scraps are unrecognizable.
3. When all 4 pass, confirm completion to unlock your batch certificate and safe plant application ratios.
